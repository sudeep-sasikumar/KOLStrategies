# Strategy: Daumen – "My New Filters (Updated)"

- **Source:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qONQbEUQx9Y (channel: Daumen, published 12 Nov 2026, length 4:51)
- **Platform used in video:** Terminal (trade.padre.gg, linked in the video description). Solana memecoins.
- **Board layout:** three columns, each with its own filter set: **New**, **Soon**, **Migrated**.
- **Scope:** The video covers token-discovery filters only. It gives no entry, exit, position-sizing or stop-loss rules.

**Stated goal of the filters:** reduce noise in the New and Soon columns without missing any runners.

Values below come from what he says and from the filter panel shown on screen. Where a value is visible on screen but not spoken, it is marked *(on screen)*.

---

## Column 1: New

| Section | Filter | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Protocols | Mayhem mode | **Hide** |
| Audit | Token age (mins) | **Max 1** |
| $ Metrics | Market cap | **Min $1,000 – Max $30,000** |
| Socials | (none) | No social filter |
| Keywords | Exclude keywords | `FREE ENTRY, READ OR ███ FREE` *(on screen; one word is censored in the video; not discussed)* |

No other filters are set on this column. The panel shows 1 active Protocols filter, 1 active Audit filter and 2 active Metrics filters.

**His reasons:**
- **Mayhem mode hidden:** he says nobody uses Mayhem mode but there are many deploys on it.
- **Token age max 1 min:** otherwise the column keeps tokens that are 4–5 minutes old, which creates noise, especially when there are spam deploys.
- **Market cap 1K–30K:** he used a 3K minimum before. He lowered it because coins can now be paired to other coins, so the starting market cap can be lower.
- **No socials filter:** he removed "at least one social". In New you want to see everything. The only filters here should handle spam deploys or remove tokens after you have already seen them.

**Tested and rejected:** Dev holding min 1%. His idea was that the dev of a good token holds some supply. He dropped it because devs sometimes make quick sells and buys on different wallets.

**Note:** He says this setup stays clean in the European time zone (his main session). The North American session is more chaotic, but still better than his previous filters.

## Column 2: Soon

| Section | Filter | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Protocols | Mayhem mode | **Hide** |
| Audit | Token age (mins) | **Max 15** |
| Audit | Dev holding | **Max 20%** *(on screen)* |
| Audit | Snipers holdings | **Max 20%** *(on screen)* |
| Audit | Holders | **Min 4** |
| $ Metrics | Volume | **Min $4,000** *(on screen)* |
| $ Metrics | Market cap | **Min $6,000** *(on screen)* |
| $ Metrics | Total fees (SOL) | **Min 0.1** |
| Socials | (none) | No social filter |
| Keywords | Exclude keywords | `FREE ENTRY, READ OR ███ FREE` *(on screen; same as New)* |

The panel shows 1 active Protocols filter, 4 active Audit filters and 3 active Metrics filters.

**His reasons:**
- **Token age max 15 min:** he had it at 25 and may go down to 10. If you trench all day you have already seen these coins. He keeps 15 so that a token he looked at and forgot gets a second look, then gets cleared once enough time has passed.
- **Dev holding and snipers limits:** "personal preference". He has used these from before.
- **Holders min 4:** extra cleanup, so the token is organic and not a major bundle. He says it does not make him miss runners.
- **Volume, market cap and total fees values:** set by trial and error and by talking to other traders. He says the previous total fees value was "05". He says these three together give tokens that reach this column a better chance of being good.
- **No socials filter:** he removed "at least one social". Many tech projects and projects on new launchpads have no socials linked but can still be good (for example, you open one and see a tracked wallet). Tokens reaching this column are already heavily filtered, so a socials filter isn't needed.

## Column 3: Migrated

| Section | Filter | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Protocols | Mayhem mode | **Hide** |
| Audit | Snipers holdings | **Max 25%** *(on screen)* |
| $ Metrics | Market cap | **Min $6,000** |
| $ Metrics | Total fees (SOL) | **Min 6** |
| Socials | (none) | No social filter |
| Keywords | Exclude keywords | *(empty)* |

The panel shows 1 active Protocols filter, 1 active Audit filter and 2 active Metrics filters.

**His reasons:**
- **Audit has only max snipers:** he used other audit filters here before. He removed them because they made him miss many good coins.
- **Total fees min 6:** raised from 4. He picked it by opening and closing many tokens to see which value works best. He may raise or lower it later.
- **No socials filter:** he says "at least one social" makes no sense here because everything is already filtered.

## General

- He says all values come from trial and error and he changes them often.
- Save the filter sets after you configure them.
